Good to know

Does my insurance pay?

Sessions on Nahbar are paid by you. Some private insurers (PKV) reimburse them, so ask yours. If you’re on statutory insurance, you can also call 116 117 to book an initial appointment with a therapist who bills your Krankenkasse.

Who are the therapists?

Psychologische Psychotherapeut:innen and ärztliche Psychotherapeut:innen with a licence (Approbation). We check every licence before a profile goes live.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is stored in the EU and handled under the GDPR (DSGVO). Video sessions are end-to-end encrypted.

Can I cancel the trial?

Any time before day 14, with one tap. You won’t be charged.

Need help right now? Call the Telefonseelsorge for free, any time: 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222. In an emergency, call 112.
